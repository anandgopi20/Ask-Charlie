## Handshake
Handshake is the main platform used by UNH for career services:
- Job and internship search
- Scheduling appointments with CDC advisors
- Internship registration for academic credit
- Career fairs, employer info sessions, and events
- Resume and cover letter submission and review
- On-campus recruiting and interview scheduling
- Access at: https://newhaven.joinhandshake.com

## Making an Appointment
- Log into Handshake and click "Career Center" → "Appointments"
- Choose appointment type: resume review, career advising, mock interview, job search
- Same-day walk-in appointments available during walk-in hours

## Resume & Cover Letter Help
- One-on-one resume review appointments available
- Resume writing guides for undergrad and graduate students
- Cover letter guide and templates
- LinkedIn profile review and optimization guide
- Drop-in resume reviews during walk-in hours

## Interview Preparation
- Mock interview appointments with CDC advisors
- Big Interview platform (free for UNH students) for practice
- Interview guides by industry available on Handshake

## Job & Internship Search
- Full-time, part-time, and internship listings on Handshake
- On-campus jobs listed through Student Employment Office
- Career fairs held each fall and spring semester
- Employer networking events throughout the year

## Internship for Academic Credit
- Register internships for academic credit through CDC
- Requires faculty sponsor and CDC approval
- Forms and process available on Handshake

## International Student Career Services (CPT / OPT)
- **CPT (Curricular Practical Training):** Work authorization during studies
- **OPT (Optional Practical Training):** Work authorization after graduation (up to 12 months)
- **STEM OPT Extension:** Additional 24 months for STEM degree graduates
- International-friendly employer resources and job listings
- Work with OISS (Office of International Students and Scholars) for visa documentation
- OISS contact: oiss@newhaven.edu | 203-932-7449

## Career Fairs & Events
- Fall Career Fair: typically held in September/October
- Spring Career Fair: typically held in February/March
- Industry-specific networking nights throughout the year
- All events listed on Handshake under "Events"
